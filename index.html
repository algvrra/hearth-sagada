📍 Sagada, Mountain Province

Explore Sagada

Nestled among the clouds at 1,500m above sea level — ancient caves, hanging coffins, pine-scented air, and a silence that restores the soul.

✨ Must-See in Sagada

⚰️
Hanging Coffins of Echo Valley
A 2,000-year-old Igorot burial tradition — coffins nailed to cliffside rock faces overlooking a misty valley.
🕯️
Sumaguing Cave
The "Big Cave" of Sagada — a spectacular underground world of stalactites, stalagmites, and ancient rock formations.
🌫️
Kiltepan Peak Sunrise
Wake up at 4AM and hike to catch a sea of clouds glowing gold as the sun rises over the Cordillera mountains.
🌊
Bomod-ok Falls
A powerful 200-foot waterfall hidden in the mountains — a 2-hour trek through rice terraces and forest trails.
🌾
Sagada Rice Terraces
Ancient hand-carved terraces cascading down mountain slopes — a living cultural landscape of the Igorot people.
☕
Sagada Coffee & Lemon Pie
Sip locally grown arabica coffee and try the legendary Sagada lemon pie — a highland tradition you can't leave without.

📌 Quick Facts

🗺️ Location: Mountain Province, CAR
🌡️ Climate: 10–22°C year-round
⛰️ Elevation: ~1,500 m above sea level
🚗 From Baguio: ~5–6 hours by bus/van
🗣️ Language: Kankana-ey, Filipino, English
📅 Best time: November – April (dry season)
